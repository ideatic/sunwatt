SunWatt

Your home solar output and the electricity price, in widgets. Free for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

PrivacySupport

What it does

  • Solar: current power (kW), today's energy (kWh) and today's curve, from Huawei FusionSolar (kiosk link), SolarEdge (site ID and API key) or PVOutput (system ID and API key; works with many inverter brands such as Fronius, SMA, Enphase, GoodWe and Growatt).
  • Electricity price: Spain (regulated PVPC, Red Eléctrica) and day-ahead wholesale prices for Portugal, France, Germany & Luxembourg, Austria, Belgium, Netherlands and North Italy.
  • Widgets: small, medium, large, Lock Screen and a Control Center control. Light, dark, tinted and clear.
  • Siri and Shortcuts, and an Apple Watch app with complications.
Private by design. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Nothing is sent to the developer.
